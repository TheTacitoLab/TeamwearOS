How to use the Order Hub

📐 Sizes tab

Enter the quantity needed for each size. Changing a number automatically adds or removes rows in the Personalisation tab. Or click Send Player Link on a product to let a player choose their own size and personalisation — their submission updates the table automatically.

✏️ Personalisation tab

Fill in each player's Full Name, personalisation type, jersey name and number. Rows show a green border when complete and a red border when information is still needed. All changes save automatically.

📋 Summary tab

See all players and their completion status at a glance. Download a Preview PDF at any time. The Download PDF button unlocks in green when every entry is complete.

🔗 Sending player links

Send All-Players Link (top right) gives one link for a player to fill in details for every product. Send Player Link on each product card gives a link just for that product. Players are asked for their Full Name before entering their details.
